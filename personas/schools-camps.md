## What this visitor is trying to do

Schools convert to an inquiry or open-house visit feeding a seasonal application cycle; camps
convert to a paid registration with real urgency because sessions sell out. The parent scans for
concrete differentiators (class size, ratios, matriculation outcomes; for camps, session dates,
ages, and prices) rather than mission language, then for tuition and financial aid (the share of
families receiving aid and the average grant), admissions steps and dates, faculty or staff bios
with credentials and tenure, accreditation, and safety (background checks, staff ratios, lifeguard
and medical staffing, health policies). The child looks for candid students or campers in action
and a day-in-the-life video, not posed brochure shots. The most common failure is a home page that
serves current families and prospects at once; it should be prospect-first with the portal one
click away. For camps, remaining spots per session and a waitlist drive the decision.

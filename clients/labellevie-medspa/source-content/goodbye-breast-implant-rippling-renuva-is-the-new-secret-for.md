## **First of All: Why Do Implants Ripple?**

Before we talk about the fix, let’s talk about why it happens. I promise, your body isn’t “rejecting” anything.

Implant rippling usually happens for a very simple, anatomical reason: a lack of natural padding.

Think of a breast implant like a water balloon. If you put a water balloon under a thick, plush down comforter, you won’t see or feel the edges of the balloon. But if you put that same water balloon under a thin silk sheet, you are going to see every single crease and ripple.

Rippling usually happens if:

- You naturally have very little breast tissue or body fat.
- You lost weight after your augmentation.
- You have saline implants (which tend to ripple more than silicone).
- The implant is placed over the muscle rather than under it.

For years, the only real solution to this was a surgical fat transfer. A surgeon would have to take you back into the OR, perform liposuction on your thighs or stomach, process that fat, and inject it around your implants.

More surgery. More anesthesia. More downtime. Ugh.

## **Enter Renuva: The “No-Lipo” Fat Transfer**

At La Belle Vie in Draper, UT, I am always hunting for aesthetic treatments that give my patients maximum results with minimum downtime. That’s why I am so incredibly passionate about Renuva.

Renuva is an injectable treatment that literally tells your body to grow its own fat.

Instead of extracting fat from your stomach via liposuction, Renuva uses an FDA-regulated, purified adipose (fat) matrix. It comes right out of a syringe. When I inject it into the areas where your implant is rippling, Renuva acts like a microscopic honeycomb scaffold.

Over the next few weeks and months, your body says, “Oh, hey! Look at this nice little home! Let’s put some of our own healthy, natural fat cells right here.”

Eventually, the Renuva matrix completely disappears, leaving behind only your own natural fat, beautifully and permanently padding the edges of your implant.

**_Mind. Blown. Right?_**

## **Why Renuva is a Dream for Implant Rippling**

As an aesthetic Nurse Practitioner (MSN, APRN, FNP-C), I am incredibly picky about what I offer my patients. Here is why Renuva is basically tailor-made for breast implant rippling:

1. **No General Anesthesia or Surgery**

You’ve already had surgery; you don’t need another one. This is an in-office injectable treatment done right here at our medical spa in Draper. You drive yourself here, and you drive yourself home.
2. **It’s Your Own Tissue**

We aren’t masking the ripples with a temporary hyaluronic acid filler. We are actually correcting the root cause of the problem (lack of fat) by helping your body build its own padding.
3. **It Feels Completely Natural**

Because the end result is your own fat, it feels soft, warm, and entirely natural. No one—not even you—will be able to tell where the implant ends and your natural tissue begins.
4. **Zero Liposuction Required**

If you’re already very lean (which is often why the rippling happened in the first place!), you might not even have enough fat to harvest for a surgical fat transfer. Renuva solves this problem completely.

# Understanding hydrafacial

![Hydrafacial](https://i0.wp.com/uvderm.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/howitworks_header.jpg?fit=1030%2C406&ssl=1)

## What is Hydrafacial?

Hydrafacial is a skin-resurfacing procedure that “sands” the outer layer of the skin to reveal healthier, smoother skin. HydraFacial uses a patented Vortex-Fusion delivery system to gently exfoliate, extract and hydrate the skin. The spiral design delivers painless extractions and leaves skin looking smooth and clear. The skin’s surface is saturated with antioxidants and peptides to maximize your glow.

![Hydrafacial](https://i0.wp.com/uvderm.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/IMG_5926-Large-e1761252031522.jpeg?fit=768%2C744&ssl=1)

## What can I expect?

**A HydraFacial only takes about 30 minutes and has zero downtime****.**You won’t experience the irritation you would have with dermabrasion procedures and can return to regular activities after treatment.

**What areas does it treat?**

- Face
- Neck
- Decolletage

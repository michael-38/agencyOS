## Jordan Harris, DO

Dr. Harris is a general dermatologist who enjoys every facet of dermatology, from [pediatrics](https://uvderm.com/providers/jordan-harris-do/) to complicated cutaneous [skin cancers](https://uvderm.com/skin-conditions/skin-cancer/). He performs in-office [Mohs micrographic surgery](https://uvderm.com/treatment/mohs-surgery/) for the removal of skin cancers, as well as excision and removal of benign tumors and [cysts](https://uvderm.com/skin-conditions/cyst/). He has the vision to offer caring, comfortable, and comprehensive dermatologic care, with the genuine goal of treating each patient as family. He is also a father to young children and uses his experience as a father to offer a unique perspective and approach to his pediatric patients.

Dr. Harris graduated with Honors from Boise State University with a Bachelor of Science in Human Biology. He received his medical degree at Pacific Northwest University of Health Science in Washington State, graduating among the top of his class, and was elected to the Sigma Sigma Phi honor society. Following medical school, he completed his internship through Michigan State University at the Oakwood Southshore Hospital. Following his internship, he completed his residency training in Dermatology and Cutaneous Surgery through Western University and Aspen Dermatology Residency Program based in Utah Valley where he served as chief resident.

Dr. Harris married his love, Jessica and they have five incredible children together. His family is the pinnacle of his life, and his love for his patients is a second!

Aging is a natural and aesthetically pleasing stage of life that brings with it valuable life experiences, cherished memories, and the joy of watching one’s family grow and evolve over time. However, as the years go by, the skin undergoes changes as well. The skin loses collagen and elasticity, resulting in increased fragility. Furthermore, a decrease in the production of hyaluronic acid, a substance that absorbs water in the skin, can cause a thinning and dull appearance. As a result, wrinkles, fine lines, age spots, and growths such as skin tags and warts, and even skin cancers can develop. In women, menopause can directly impact hormone levels, further altering the quality, texture, and elasticity of the skin.

Although these changes are a natural part of aging, it is possible to stimulate collagen production and increase hyaluronic acid to maintain a youthful, healthy, and radiant appearance.

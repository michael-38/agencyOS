From a phone call to a lit sign

Six steps, and you see a proof and a fixed price before anything is built.

  1. Site survey

    We come out, measure the frontage or the vehicle, photograph it, and check the power and the zoning overlay.

  2. Design proof

    A rendering of the sign on a photograph of your own building, with a fixed itemised price. Two revisions included.

  3. Permit

    We draw and file with the City of Tacoma or your jurisdiction, and handle any correction notices. Two to four weeks.

  4. Fabrication

    Built in our shop: routed aluminium, LED modules, UL-listed wiring. We send a photo the day it comes off the bench.

  5. Installation

    Our own crew and our own bucket truck, scheduled around your opening hours. Most installs take half a day.

  6. Warranty & service

    Five years on electrical parts and labour, three on wraps, written into the quote. One number to call if anything fails.

Frequently asked questions

Cost, permits, timing, warranty and leased vehicles: the seven things we get asked on almost every first call.

How much does a storefront sign cost?

Illuminated channel letters on a typical retail frontage run $4,500 to $18,000 depending on letter height, depth and how many circuits the electrician has to pull. A lit cabinet sign is usually $3,000 to $9,000, and non-illuminated dimensional letters start around $1,400. Every quote is itemised into design, fabrication, permit and installation so you can see what is driving the number.

Do you handle the sign permit?

Yes, on every exterior job. We produce the permit drawings, file with the City of Tacoma or the relevant Pierce County jurisdiction, and deal with any correction notices. Permits typically add two to four weeks, and we tell you at the survey if your address sits in a design-review overlay that will add more.

How long does the whole thing take?

Most storefront signs are installed three to five weeks after you approve the proof, plus permit time. Vehicle wraps take five working days from art approval, and we need the vehicle for two of them. Banners and window graphics are usually ready in two to three business days.

Can you repair a sign you did not build?

Yes. We relamp, re-power and repair signs from any manufacturer, and we carry a bucket truck rated to 45 feet. Service calls within Pierce County are $185 for the first hour and $110 an hour after that, plus parts.

Will the colours match our brand exactly?

We match to Pantone rather than to a screenshot, and we will print a physical swatch for you to hold against your existing materials before anything goes into production. Bring your brand guide if you have one, or a printed piece if you do not.

Can you wrap a leased vehicle?

Usually, yes. We use cast vinyl with a removable adhesive on leased vehicles, which comes off cleanly at the end of the term without damaging factory paint. Check your lease for a graphics clause first, and we will remove the wrap for you before you hand the vehicle back.

What warranty do you give?

Five years on electrical components and labour for signs we fabricate and install, three years on vehicle wraps against lifting, cracking and fading, and one year on banners and window graphics. The warranty is written into the quote, not buried on the back of an invoice.

## A death has occurred. Here is exactly what to do.

Call us at (610) 555-0107 and we will take it from there. If you want to know what happens next, these are the five steps in order.

**Call the right number first:** If the death was expected and hospice is involved, call hospice. If it was unexpected, or you are unsure, call 911. In a hospital or nursing home, tell the nurse. Then call us. You do not need to wait for anyone’s permission.

**Call us at (610) 555-0107:** A funeral director answers, at any hour. We will ask where your person is, their name, and your name. That is all we need to begin.

**We come and bring them into our care:** Usually within two hours, day or night, in an unmarked vehicle. Two of our staff come. You are welcome to be present or not, whichever you need.

**We meet the next day, not tonight:** Arrangements take about ninety minutes, at our house or at yours. Nothing is decided tonight beyond burial or cremation, and even that can usually wait until morning.

**We handle the paperwork:** Death certificates, permits, Social Security notification, veterans’ benefits, the newspaper notice and the online obituary. We order certified copies for you. Most families need between six and ten.

**What to bring to the arrangement meeting:** None of this has to be perfect, and nothing is urgent enough to search the house at 3 a.m.

## Frequently asked questions

### Someone has died at home. What do I do right now?

Call us at (610) 555-0107. A funeral director answers, day or night. If the death was expected and hospice is involved, call hospice first and then us; if it was unexpected, call 911 first. You do not have to decide anything else tonight. We will come, bring your person into our care, and talk about arrangements tomorrow.

### How much does a funeral cost?

Direct cremation at Alderman & Sons is $1,895, cremation with a memorial service is $3,650, a celebration of life is $2,980, green burial is $4,200, and a traditional burial service is $7,450. Cemetery charges, vaults, monuments, flowers and newspaper notices are paid to other companies and are additional. Our full General Price List is downloadable from this page.

### How soon do we have to decide anything?

Only two decisions are urgent: whether to bring your person into our care, and whether burial or cremation. Everything else (the day, the service, the casket, the obituary) can wait a day or two, and families who wait a day usually make better decisions. Pennsylvania places no deadline that requires you to decide overnight.

### What should I bring to the arrangement meeting?

Bring a recent photograph, clothing for your person if there will be a viewing, and any pre-arrangement or insurance paperwork. For the death certificate we need their full legal name, date and place of birth, Social Security number, parents' names including mother's maiden name, education, and occupation. If they served, bring the DD-214 if you can find it. If you cannot, we will request it.

### Can we still have a service if we choose cremation?

Yes, and most families who choose cremation do. You may have a viewing before the cremation, a memorial service after it with the urn present, or a celebration of life weeks later when relatives can travel. Cremation is a choice about disposition, not a decision to skip the gathering.

### Do you handle veterans' benefits?

Yes, at no additional charge. We file for the VA burial allowance, request the burial flag and the government headstone or marker, coordinate the honor guard, and arrange interment at Washington Crossing National Cemetery. A veteran's burial in a national cemetery, including the grave, the liner and perpetual care, is at no cost to the family.

### Can family who cannot travel watch the service?

Yes. Every service at Alderman & Sons is livestreamed at no charge on a private link you can share, and the recording stays available for twelve months so anyone who missed it can watch later.

### Do we have to embalm?

No. Pennsylvania does not require embalming. It is generally needed only for a public viewing, for transport by some airlines, or where there will be a delay before burial. Refrigeration is available, and green burial does not permit embalming at all. We will tell you plainly whether your plan needs it.

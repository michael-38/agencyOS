Every price, published

These are the prices Juniper charges, per unit, per syringe or per session. They are the same numbers your provider quotes in the room, and no membership is required to see them.

Botox Cosmetic

Neuromodulator for forehead lines, the glabellar “11s” and crow’s feet. Onset around day three, full effect near day fourteen.

What to expect · For lines that appear when you move, not lines etched at rest. Ten minutes in the chair and no downtime; onset around day three, full effect day fourteen, wearing off over three to four months.

$13 / unitForehead + glabella, 30-45 units

Dysport

An alternative neuromodulator that spreads a little more, which some patients prefer across the forehead.

What to expect · Same candidacy as Botox. No downtime; onset around day two, full effect day fourteen, wearing off over three to four months.

$4.50 / unitDysport units, not Botox units

Dermal filler

Juvéderm, Restylane and RHA hyaluronic acid filler for cheeks, lips, chin, jawline and tear troughs.

What to expect · For volume and structure rather than fine lines alone. Expect swelling for two to five days and possible bruising for a week; the final shape settles around week four.

From $725 / syringeReversible with hyaluronidase

Sculptra

A poly-L-lactic acid collagen stimulator for gradual volume, usually three vials spaced a month apart.

What to expect · For gradual collagen loss across the whole face, not for a single crease. No visible downtime; the change builds over three to six months and is judged after the third vial.

$850 / vial

Moxi laser resurfacing

Sciton Moxi, a gentle non-ablative fractional laser for tone, early sun damage and texture. Safe in summer with sun care.

What to expect · Suitable for every skin type, including tanned skin. Pink for one to three days and a sandpaper texture for about four; tone evens out over three to four weeks.

$600 / sessionSeries of three, $1,650

BBL Hero

Sciton broadband light for brown spots, redness and broken capillaries on the face, chest, arms and hands.

What to expect · For brown spots, redness and broken capillaries, not for active tan. Like a mild sunburn for a day; spots darken then flake over a week. Usually two or three sessions, four weeks apart.

From $450 / area

Microneedling with PRF

Collagen induction with platelet-rich fibrin drawn from your own blood on site, for texture and acne scarring.

What to expect · For texture and boxcar or rolling scars, not ice-pick scars. Red for a day or two and flaky for three; collagen change shows from week six and is best judged at three months.

$650 / sessionSeries of three, $1,750

HydraFacial

Cleanse, extract and hydrate, with optional boosters. No downtime, so it suits the week of an event.

What to expect · For dull skin in the week of an event. No downtime at all, make-up the same afternoon, and the glow lasts about a week.

From $199

CoolSculpting Elite

Cryolipolysis for pockets of fat under the chin, on the flanks and on the abdomen, priced per applicator cycle.

What to expect · For pinchable fat that has not moved with training; it is not a weight-loss treatment. Numbness and swelling for one to two weeks; change is visible at eight weeks and settled at twelve.

From $750 / cycle

Emsculpt Neo

Radiofrequency plus electromagnetic muscle stimulation for abdomen and flanks, sold as a course of four.

What to expect · For abdominal and flank tone, and not suitable with certain implants or devices. No downtime beyond delayed soreness for a day or two; results are assessed twelve weeks after the fourth session.

$3,200 / course of 4

What we use, and what happens if something goes wrong

Juniper treats only with FDA-approved injectables and FDA-cleared devices bought direct from the manufacturer, and keeps hyaluronidase on site so filler can be dissolved the same day if it needs to be.

Protocols

  • Every treatment plan is reviewed by Elena Marchetti, MD before the first session, and again before any change in product or dose.
  • Hyaluronidase is stocked on site for same-day reversal of hyaluronic acid filler, along with a written vascular occlusion protocol the whole clinical team rehearses twice a year.
  • Injectables are purchased direct from Allergan, Galderma and Revance, never from a grey-market distributor, and every lot number is recorded in your chart.
  • Standardised photography at a fixed distance and light before and after every course, kept in your chart whether or not you consent to publication.
  • A medical history and medication review at every visit, because anticoagulants, recent dental work and an upcoming event all change the plan.

Frequently asked questions

The eight questions our front desk is asked most often, answered in full.

How do I know which treatment is right for my concern?

Start from the concern rather than the treatment name. Each concern page lists the treatments Juniper uses for it, what each one does differently, and roughly what a course looks like. If you are still unsure, book a thirty-minute consultation. It is $75, credited toward any treatment booked within sixty days.

What does Botox cost at Juniper Aesthetics?

Botox Cosmetic is $13 per unit and Dysport is $4.50 per Dysport unit. A forehead and glabella treatment is commonly 30 to 45 Botox units, so most patients pay between $390 and $585. Your provider counts the units with you before treating.

Who actually performs the injections?

Noor Haddad, RN, BSN and Priya Raman, NP-C perform all injectable treatments. Elena Marchetti, MD, a board-certified dermatologist, is the medical director, reviews every treatment plan, and is on site Tuesdays and Thursdays.

Is there downtime after Moxi or BBL Hero?

Most patients are pink for one to three days after Moxi and describe a sandpaper texture for about four days as the treated skin sheds. BBL Hero usually looks like a mild sunburn for a day, and brown spots darken before flaking off over the following week.

How long do filler results last?

Hyaluronic acid filler is gradually metabolised by the body. Depending on the product and the area treated, most patients return between nine and eighteen months later. Lips generally need refreshing sooner than cheeks or jawline.

Can I pay over time?

Yes. Juniper accepts CareCredit with six, twelve and eighteen-month promotional plans, and Cherry, which runs a soft credit check that does not affect your credit score. Both can be approved in the treatment room.

What happens at a consultation?

Thirty minutes with a provider: your history and medications, a look at your skin under magnification, standardised photographs at a fixed distance and light, and a written plan with prices and a suggested order. Nothing is treated the same day unless you ask and the provider agrees you are a candidate.

Do you treat men?

Yes. Roughly one in five Juniper patients is a man, most often for neuromodulator, jawline filler, BBL Hero for sun damage, and Emsculpt Neo.

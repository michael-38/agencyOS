## What brought you in?

Most people arrive knowing the problem, not the product, so start from the concern and let the provider match the treatment, or jump straight to the treatment list if you already know its name.

**Fine lines & wrinkles:** Forehead, glabellar “11s”, crow’s feet and lip lines, treated with neuromodulator and, where the line is etched at rest, resurfacing.

**Lost volume:** Flattened cheeks, hollowing under the eye, a softening jawline. Filler for structure, Sculptra where collagen is the underlying story.

**Sun damage & redness:** Brown spots, broken capillaries and the diffuse redness Colorado sun leaves behind, treated with broadband light.

**Texture & pores:** Rough surface, enlarged pores and dull tone, treated with fractional resurfacing or collagen induction.

**Acne & acne scarring:** Active breakouts and the boxcar or rolling scars left behind, usually a staged course rather than one visit.

**Body contouring:** Pockets of fat that have not moved with training, and muscle tone in the abdomen and flanks.

**Dull, tired skin:** An event in two weeks and skin that is not cooperating. A facial with extractions and boosters, no downtime.

**Not sure yet:** Bring the concern, not a treatment name. Thirty minutes, photographs, a written plan, and the fee credited if you book.

## Every price, published

These are the prices Juniper charges, per unit, per syringe or per session. They are the same numbers your provider quotes in the room, and no membership is required to see them.

**Botox Cosmetic:** Neuromodulator for forehead lines, the glabellar “11s” and crow’s feet. Onset around day three, full effect near day fourteen.

**Dysport:** An alternative neuromodulator that spreads a little more, which some patients prefer across the forehead.

**Dermal filler:** Juvéderm, Restylane and RHA hyaluronic acid filler for cheeks, lips, chin, jawline and tear troughs.

**Sculptra:** A poly-L-lactic acid collagen stimulator for gradual volume, usually three vials spaced a month apart.

**Moxi laser resurfacing:** Sciton Moxi, a gentle non-ablative fractional laser for tone, early sun damage and texture. Safe in summer with sun care.

**BBL Hero:** Sciton broadband light for brown spots, redness and broken capillaries on the face, chest, arms and hands.

**Microneedling with PRF:** Collagen induction with platelet-rich fibrin drawn from your own blood on site, for texture and acne scarring.

**HydraFacial:** Cleanse, extract and hydrate, with optional boosters. No downtime, so it suits the week of an event.

**CoolSculpting Elite:** Cryolipolysis for pockets of fat under the chin, on the flanks and on the abdomen, priced per applicator cycle.

**Emsculpt Neo:** Radiofrequency plus electromagnetic muscle stimulation for abdomen and flanks, sold as a course of four.

**Consultation:** Thirty minutes with a provider: history, magnified skin assessment, standardised photographs and a written plan.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I know which treatment is right for my concern?

Start from the concern rather than the treatment name. Each concern page lists the treatments Juniper uses for it, what each one does differently, and roughly what a course looks like. If you are still unsure, book a thirty-minute consultation. It is $75, credited toward any treatment booked within sixty days.

### What does Botox cost at Juniper Aesthetics?

Botox Cosmetic is $13 per unit and Dysport is $4.50 per Dysport unit. A forehead and glabella treatment is commonly 30 to 45 Botox units, so most patients pay between $390 and $585. Your provider counts the units with you before treating.

### Who actually performs the injections?

Noor Haddad, RN, BSN and Priya Raman, NP-C perform all injectable treatments. Elena Marchetti, MD, a board-certified dermatologist, is the medical director, reviews every treatment plan, and is on site Tuesdays and Thursdays.

### Is there downtime after Moxi or BBL Hero?

Most patients are pink for one to three days after Moxi and describe a sandpaper texture for about four days as the treated skin sheds. BBL Hero usually looks like a mild sunburn for a day, and brown spots darken before flaking off over the following week.

### How long do filler results last?

Hyaluronic acid filler is gradually metabolised by the body. Depending on the product and the area treated, most patients return between nine and eighteen months later. Lips generally need refreshing sooner than cheeks or jawline.

### Can I pay over time?

Yes. Juniper accepts CareCredit with six, twelve and eighteen-month promotional plans, and Cherry, which runs a soft credit check that does not affect your credit score. Both can be approved in the treatment room.

### What happens at a consultation?

Thirty minutes with a provider: your history and medications, a look at your skin under magnification, standardised photographs at a fixed distance and light, and a written plan with prices and a suggested order. Nothing is treated the same day unless you ask and the provider agrees you are a candidate.

### Do you treat men?

Yes. Roughly one in five Juniper patients is a man, most often for neuromodulator, jawline filler, BBL Hero for sun damage, and Emsculpt Neo.

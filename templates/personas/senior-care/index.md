## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Meadowvale cost each month?

Independent living starts at $3,450 a month, assisted living at $4,900 plus a care tier between $450 and $1,850, and memory care at The Bridges is $6,800 all-inclusive. There is a one-time community fee of $2,500 and a second-person fee of $850 a month. There is no buy-in and no entrance deposit, and residency is month to month with thirty days' notice.

### What happens when my parent needs more help than they do now?

Care is re-assessed at move-in, at ninety days, twice a year after that, and any time something changes. If the assessment moves your parent to a higher tier we tell you the new monthly figure in writing before it takes effect. Moving from independent to assisted living, or from assisted living into The Bridges, happens inside the same building with the same dining room and the same staff.

### Does Medicaid pay for assisted living in Colorado?

Colorado's Home and Community Based Services waiver for the Elderly, Blind and Disabled can cover the care portion of assisted living for residents who qualify financially and clinically, but it does not cover room and board. Meadowvale holds a limited number of waiver apartments in assisted living; The Bridges is private pay only. Our director will tell you on the tour how many waiver apartments are open.

### Can VA benefits help pay for care?

Aid and Attendance is a monthly addition to a VA pension for wartime veterans and surviving spouses who need help with daily activities. It is paid to the resident, not to us, and most families we see use it against the care tier. Applications commonly take several months, so start before the move rather than after.

### Will long-term care insurance cover this?

Most long-term care policies pay a daily or monthly benefit once a resident needs help with two or more activities of daily living, after an elimination period that is often ninety days. Our business office completes the provider sections of the claim, sends monthly care logs directly to the carrier, and will read your policy with you before you move in.

### What are the staffing ratios, and is a nurse always here?

Assisted living runs one caregiver to eight residents from 7 am to 3 pm, one to eleven from 3 pm to 11 pm, and one to fifteen overnight. The Bridges runs one to five during the day and one to eight overnight. A licensed nurse is on site twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week.

### Can I see the state inspection results?

Yes. Meadowvale is licensed by the Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment as assisted living residence ALR-2189, and every survey is public on the CDPHE Health Facilities site, which we link to from this page. Our most recent survey, in March 2026, cited no deficiencies.

### How do I know it is time to move a parent?

The signs families describe to us most often are a fall or a near fall, missed medications, weight loss, unopened mail piling up, a spouse doing care they can no longer manage, and a hospital discharge with no safe plan for home. None of them alone decides it. Call and describe the week you have had; our director will tell you honestly whether it sounds like a respite stay, assisted living, or not yet.

### Can we bring our own furniture, and can pets come?

Apartments are unfurnished so that residents bring their own furniture, and we ask families to bring what is familiar rather than what is new. Cats and dogs under forty pounds are welcome with a one-time $400 pet fee, provided the resident or the family can care for them.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a house cleaning cost in Asheville?

A standard clean is a flat rate set by bedrooms and bathrooms: $129 for a one-bed, one-bath home up to $269 for a five-bed, three-bath home. Deep cleans and move-out cleans have their own flat rates, and recurring visits take 10% to 20% off.

### Do I have to be home during the clean?

No. Most clients give us a door code or a lockbox and go to work. You choose an arrival window when you book, and your cleaner texts when she is on the way and again when she leaves.

### Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes, everything including vacuums, microfibre and Green Seal-certified products. If you would rather we used your products, leave them out and note it in your booking.

### Are your cleaners background-checked?

Every cleaner passes a Checkr national criminal background check and a Buncombe County records check before a first shift, and re-runs annually. All 22 cleaners are W-2 employees, not contractors, and Marigold carries a $1,000,000 bond and general liability policy.

### Will I get the same cleaner every time?

Yes. Recurring clients are assigned one cleaner who keeps that route. If she is out sick or on holiday we tell you in advance and send a named backup who has your notes.

### What happens if I am not happy with the clean?

Tell us within 24 hours and we re-clean whatever was missed at no charge, usually the next business day. If the re-clean still misses, that visit is refunded.

### What is the difference between a standard clean and a deep clean?

A standard clean covers the surfaces you see and touch every day. A deep clean adds baseboards, door frames, light fixtures, cabinet fronts, window sills, and moving what can be moved to clean behind it. First-time clients on a home that has not been professionally cleaned in six months usually start with a deep clean.

### Do you clean offices and short-term rentals?

Yes. Commercial work is quoted from a walkthrough rather than the flat-rate table, because square footage, frequency and access decide the price. We clean offices, dental and medical suites, salons and short-term rentals across Buncombe County.

### How do I skip, reschedule or cancel a visit?

From the booking link in your confirmation email, up to 24 hours before the visit, at no cost. Inside 24 hours there is a $45 late-cancellation fee because the cleaner has already lost the slot.

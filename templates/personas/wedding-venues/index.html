The Stone Barn

An 1840s dairy barn taken back to the stone, with a 26-foot ceiling, original hand-hewn beams, and eight-foot doors on the west wall that open onto the river. Heated and air-conditioned; string lighting and chandeliers are installed and included.

  • Seated dinner220 guests
  • Standing reception300 guests
  • Footprint80′ × 60′ · 4,800 sq ft
  • Ceiling26′ at the ridge
  • Layouts run here22 rounds of 10 with a 20′ × 20′ floor; or 12 long tables seating 180

The Orangery

A heated glass house built against the south wall of the walled garden, with a fig tree that predates the venue. Used year round: it is the whole wedding for a micro-wedding, and the cocktail hour or the rain-plan dinner for a larger one.

  • Standing reception140 guests
  • Footprint50′ × 38′ · 1,900 sq ft
  • ClimateHeated and ventilated, open all four seasons

The River Meadow & The Carriage House

Inclusions & vendors

What the site fee covers, and who you may hire

The site fee includes the furniture, the linens, the coordinator, the parking and the rain plan; catering comes from a list of seven, bar service is ours, and every other vendor is entirely your choice.

Included in every site fee

  • Ten hours on the estate, with setup access from 10 am
  • Thirty 60″ round tables and twelve 8′ banquet tables
  • 260 Chiavari chairs in natural, gold or white
  • House linens and napkins in ivory, white or slate
  • A venue coordinator for twelve hours on the day, plus two planning meetings
  • On-site parking for 120 cars and a shuttle turnaround
  • Setup and breakdown of all estate-owned furniture
  • Both getting-ready suites from 10 am
  • The Stone Barn held as a rain plan, at no extra charge
  • A one-hour rehearsal the day before, subject to the calendar

Open vendor

Florals, photography, video, music and DJ, planning, rentals, officiant, hair and make-up and transport are all yours to choose, with no fee, no commission and no approval process. We ask only for a certificate of insurance two weeks out.

Catering from a list of seven

Ravenhill has no in-house kitchen team, so dinner comes from one of seven caterers who are licensed, insured and have worked the barn kitchen before. The list spans about $95 to $190 a head and includes two kosher-capable and one fully plant-based kitchen. You contract with them directly.

Bar service in-house

Ravenhill holds a New York State liquor licence, so all alcohol is bought and poured by us with TIPS-certified bartenders. You choose the list; outside alcohol, including toasting bottles, is not permitted, and unopened stock is not charged.

Frequently asked questions

The rain plan, the curfew, the alcohol rule, accessibility and where guests sleep, answered here rather than on a tour.

What is the rain plan?

The Stone Barn is held for every outdoor ceremony at no extra charge, and it seats your full guest count. The call is made together at 10 am on the wedding day, and the barn is flipped by our team in under an hour. You never pay a weather deposit and you never have to book a backup tent.

What time does the music have to stop?

Amplified music outdoors ends at 10 pm under the Town of Rhinebeck noise ordinance. Inside the Stone Barn, with the doors closed, music runs to 11 pm, and the estate must be clear by midnight. Extra hours before the ceremony are $850 each; the end times are fixed.

Can we bring our own alcohol?

No. Ravenhill holds a New York State liquor licence and all bar service is in-house, poured by our own TIPS-certified bartenders. You choose the list and the package, we buy and serve it, and unopened stock is not charged. Outside alcohol, including toasting bottles and welcome bags, is not permitted.

Is the estate accessible?

Yes. The Stone Barn, Orangery and Carriage House all have step-free entrances, the ceremony meadow has a compacted stone path from the parking area to the aisle, and there are two accessible restrooms in the barn. Golf carts run guests from parking to any point on the estate on request.

Where do our guests stay?

The Wynkoop Inn is five minutes away and holds room blocks for Ravenhill weddings; the Riverbend Hotel in Rhinecliff is twelve minutes and sits by the Amtrak station; Hollow Road Farmhouse sleeps sixteen and is often taken by the wedding party. We send the full lodging sheet with every tour confirmation.

How many guests can Ravenhill hold?

260 seated for a ceremony in the River Meadow, 220 seated for dinner in the Stone Barn, 300 standing in the barn, and 90 seated in the Orangery. Weddings over 220 are seated across the barn and the orangery terrace.

Do we have to use your caterer?

Catering comes from a preferred list of seven companies who are licensed, insured and have worked the barn kitchen before. Everything else (florals, photography, music, planning, rentals, officiant, hair and make-up) is fully open vendor with no fee and no approval process.

How quickly do you answer a date inquiry?

Within one business day, and the reply tells you whether your date is actually open, what the site fee is for that date, and two tour times. If the date has gone we say so in the first line rather than asking you to book a tour to find out.

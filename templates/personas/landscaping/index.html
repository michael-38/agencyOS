From first call to final walkthrough

A design-and-build project runs through five steps, and you know the full price at step three, before anyone digs.

  1. Tell us the job

    Call, or send the address and a few photos through the form. We reply the same business day.

  2. Site visit

    An estimator walks the property with you for 60-90 minutes, measures, and checks grade and equipment access.

  3. Design & fixed price

    You get a measured plan and a written proposal with one number on it, within five business days.

  4. Build

    One crew, start to finish, with a scheduled start date and a daily clean-up. Changes are re-quoted in writing first.

  5. Walkthrough

    We walk it with you, hand over care notes, and register the three-year workmanship warranty.

Frequently asked questions

These are the eight questions our estimators are asked most often on a first phone call.

Do you work in my town?

We work in Chagrin Falls, Bainbridge Township, Moreland Hills, Gates Mills, Hunting Valley, Solon, Novelty and Russell Township. If you are just outside that ring, call and ask. We will tell you honestly whether the drive makes sense.

Do you handle the design as well as the build?

Yes. Every design-and-build project starts with a measured site plan drawn in-house, and the same crew that reviews the plan with you installs it. We do not subcontract hardscape.

Can I get a quote without an in-person visit?

For mowing and seasonal clean-ups, yes. Send the address and we price from the lot size. For patios, walls and drainage we need to stand on the ground first, because grade and access change the number.

What does a paver patio cost in Chagrin Falls?

Paver patios start at $14,500 for roughly 350 square feet of standard paver on a compacted base. Larger patios, seat walls, fire pits and permeable systems price above that, and your proposal names a fixed number before anything is dug.

Do you take on recurring maintenance as well as one-off projects?

Yes, and they are separate signups. Maintenance plans run April 1 through November 30, are billed monthly, and can be cancelled with thirty days' notice.

Are you licensed and insured?

Hollow Creek carries $2,000,000 in general liability plus Ohio workers' compensation, and holds Ohio Department of Agriculture pesticide applicator licence C-48219. We will email both certificates before the first visit if you ask.

What happens at the first site visit?

One of our two estimators walks the property with you for sixty to ninety minutes, measures, checks drainage and access for equipment, and photographs the area. You get the written proposal within five business days.

Do you work through the winter?

Hardscape installation continues until the ground freezes, usually into December, and design work runs all winter. From November through March our crews are on snow and ice routes.

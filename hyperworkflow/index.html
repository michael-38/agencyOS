Before you book

The three doubts we hear most.

01

“Our work is too complicated to automate.”

We invest the time to observe how your team works, and we build agents that take most (if not all) of the work off your hands.

02

“My team is too busy for this.”

We come to them. The first two days we watch them do their normal work, so it costs attention, not extra hours. After that, plan on about two hours a day.

03

“What if the agent gets it wrong?”

We include a human in the loop to ensure the work of our agents is reviewed by you. Our agents also learn to self-improve, so they become experts over time.

How the sprint works

Ten working days. One workflow shipped.

Process diagrams don't show how the work really gets done. So we start next to your team, not in a slide deck.

Days 1 to 2

Watch the work

We sit with the person doing the work and watch every step. We ask questions and write down how the job really gets done.

Day 3

Rank what matters

Three principles behind every sprint.

What we've learned shipping agents inside real teams.

Automate workflows, not tasks.

The big wins come from fixing the whole workflow: cutting handoffs, dropping approvals nobody reads, replacing old tools. Speeding up one step barely moves anything. The workflow is the unit of automation.

You can't see the best ideas from outside.

The best things to automate never make it into a requirements doc. They only show up when you sit next to the person doing the work. So every sprint starts by watching, not scoping.

Teach the pattern, then hand it over.

Two weeks gets you the first win. The bigger payoff comes later, when your team redesigns its own work and spends the saved hours on things it could never get to before.

The sprint

What is the Agentic Implementation program?

A two-week sprint with the people who actually do the work. We watch the real job, rank what's worth automating, build the agent with your team, and ship on day 10.

Why two weeks?

Long enough to watch, build, and test. Short enough to force decisions. The point is to get a real win live, not to write a report.

What you get

What do we walk away with?

A working agent, live. A ranked list of what to automate next. Proof it works on real work. And a team that can do it again.

What happens after the sprint?

Whatever you want. Run another sprint, keep us on, or take it in-house. We plan the handoff from day one.

Do we own what you build?

Yes. The code, the prompts, the integrations, the data, and the accounts. No lock-in.

Practicalities

What about security and data access?

We take the least access we need, work inside your environment where we can, and keep a human reviewing what the agent does. We'll go through the details on the call.

How much does it cost?

We scope it on the call. The first call is free, and you'll leave with a straight answer either way.

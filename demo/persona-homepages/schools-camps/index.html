What is actually different here

Harborlight is small on purpose: one section per grade below Grade 3, two above it, and a faculty that stays. That is why a Grade 8 teacher can tell you what a child was like in Grade 2.

Ages 5-7Kindergarten - Grade 2

One section a year, 15 children, a full-time assistant teacher, daily Spanish and two recesses.

 Ages 8-10Grades 3 - 5

Two sections, 16 each, subject specialists for science, music and art, and the first harbour science block.

 Ages 11-13Grades 6 - 8

Latin from Grade 6, a 1:1 advisor from Grade 5, afternoon athletics and the secondary-school search.

Six one-week sessions in June and July, open to children from any school. $565 a week.

Every student in Grades 5 to 8 has one faculty advisor who follows them until graduation, meets them weekly, and is the single person a parent calls when something is off.

Admissions

Six steps, and the dates they fall on

Applying for 2027-28 takes about six weeks of a family’s attention between October and January, and the whole calendar is below.

  1. 18 Oct & 15 Nov 2026

    Open house. Two Saturdays, 9:30 am to noon. Classroom visits, a student-led tour, and time with the Head of School and both division heads. Children welcome. No registration needed, though it helps us set out enough coffee.

  2. October - December

    Family visit. A weekday morning on campus with an admissions officer, sitting in on two classes in the grade your child would enter. Booked individually; we run four a week, no more.

  3. By 9 January 2027

    Application. Online form, a $75 fee (waived with a financial aid application), two teacher references and the last two years of school reports. Financial aid applications are due the same day through Clarity.

  4. January - February

    Student visit day. Your child spends a morning in class with their would-be grade, does a short reading and maths assessment, and has lunch. Parents have a separate conversation with the division head.

  5. 10 March 2027

    Decisions and aid awards released together, by email at 4 pm. Every admitted family is told what they will pay the same afternoon.

  6. 24 March 2027

    Enrolment contracts due, with a 10% deposit. After this date we admit on a rolling basis wherever a place remains. Call and ask; there is often room in Grades 4 and 6.

Who is with your child, and what they cleared

Every adult on the Harborlight campus (teacher, coach, bus driver, regular volunteer) clears CORI and SAFIS background checks with fingerprinting before a first day, and the campus is locked from 8:15 to 3:15.

Accreditation

Accredited by the New England Association of Schools and Colleges since 1979, most recently reaccredited in 2024 following a two-year self-study and a four-day visiting-committee review. Member of the Association of Independent Schools in New England.

Background checks

  • CORI and SAFIS checks with fingerprinting, re-run every three years
  • Mandated-reporter training for all employees, annually
  • A two-adult rule for any student contact, including transport

Health on campus

A full-time registered nurse in the health office every school day, with written anaphylaxis, asthma, concussion and seizure protocols, an on-campus AED on each floor, and a nut-aware lunch programme run by our own kitchen.

On the water

Harbour science and sailing run under American Red Cross-certified lifeguards at one to ten in the water, with a powered safety boat on the water whenever dinghies are out and a swim assessment for every student before their first session.

What a camp day looks like

Morning meeting on the lawn at 8:45, two activity blocks, lunch on the dock, a swim, and a final block before pickup. Counsellor ratio is one to eight, and every waterfront block runs at one to ten in the water with a certified lifeguard.

Who staffs it

Twenty-two counsellors, eleven of them Harborlight faculty and the rest returning alumni over eighteen. All clear the same CORI and SAFIS checks as school staff, and an EMT is on site whenever camp is running.

Why families start here

About half of each summer’s campers come from other North Shore schools. Plenty of families use a camp week as a low-pressure way to see whether Harborlight suits their child before they ever fill in an application.

Frequently asked questions

Cost, deadlines, class size, placement, safety and camp: the eight things the admissions office is asked every week.

What does Harborlight cost, and how much aid is available?

Tuition for 2027-28 is $28,400 in Kindergarten through Grade 2, $31,200 in Grades 3 to 5, and $33,900 in Grades 6 to 8. Thirty-eight per cent of families receive need-based financial aid, the average grant is $19,600, and the total aid budget is $1.9 million. Aid is need-based only; there are no merit scholarships.

What are the admissions deadlines?

Open houses are Saturday 18 October and Saturday 15 November 2026. Applications close 9 January 2027, student visit days run through January and February, decisions are released 10 March, and enrolment contracts are due 24 March. After that we admit on a rolling basis wherever a place remains.

How big are classes, and what is the student-teacher ratio?

The average class is 14 students, capped at 16 in Grades 3 to 8 and 15 in Kindergarten through Grade 2, where there is also a full-time assistant teacher. Across the school the student-teacher ratio is 7:1.

Where do Harborlight students go after Grade 8?

Over the last five graduating classes, 96 per cent of students were placed at their first or second-choice secondary school. Graduates most often go on to Marblehead High School, Governor’s Academy, Pingree, St. John’s Prep, Brooks and Phillips Academy. Our Grade 8 dean runs the secondary-school search with every family from the autumn of Grade 7.

How do you keep children safe?

Harborlight is accredited by the New England Association of Schools and Colleges. Every employee and regular volunteer clears CORI and SAFIS background checks with fingerprinting before a first day on campus, a two-adult rule applies to all student contact, the campus is locked during the school day, and a full-time registered nurse is on site with anaphylaxis and concussion protocols.

What are the camp sessions, ages and prices?

Harborlight Summer runs six one-week sessions from 21 June to 30 July 2027 for ages 5 to 13. A week is $565, with optional extended day until 5:30 pm for $95 more. Waterfront weeks include sailing and swimming with American Red Cross-certified lifeguards at one to ten in the water; the general counsellor ratio is one to eight.

Do I have to be a Harborlight family to attend camp?

No. About half of each summer’s campers come from other schools across the North Shore, and many families use camp as a low-pressure way to see whether the school is a fit before they apply.

Is there bus or after-school care?

Morning care opens at 7:45 and after-school care runs to 5:30 every day the school is open, at $12 an hour or $2,400 for the year. Shared vans run from Salem, Beverly and Swampscott for $1,850 a year.
